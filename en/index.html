How term work actually moves

The office places a person in a group and the group on a subject. After that the path is fixed: a slot on the timetable, a lesson, a submission or a quiz, a mark in the gradebook.

01
The office issues the account

There is no self-enrolment. A record is created, the student is placed in a group, the group is attached to subjects.

02
The timetable carries room and time

A lesson lasts 45 minutes. A remote slot holds an outside link, not a built-in video call.

03
Lesson, submission, quiz

A lesson holds text, a file, or a link. Homework is turned in as text or a file; a quiz is marked at once.

04
The mark lands in the gradebook

For an assignment, a quiz, or work in the room. A student sees their own marks, never anyone else’s.

Three desks, no shop window
